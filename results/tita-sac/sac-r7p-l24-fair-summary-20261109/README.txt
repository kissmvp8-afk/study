L24 versus L48: 40 matched evaluation pairs
Four fixed candidates, five seeds, D and native-std S. D/S are not independent training seeds. The comparison checks matching source, initial physics, RNG and evaluation protocol, with frozen-state and model SHA evidence.
Original C1 L48 scores are read without rescoring or replacement. Incomplete trajectories keep whole-arm continuous averages NULL; survivor-only averages are not used.
This is controlled simulation evidence, not proof of stable real-robot operation, a causal conclusion, or permission to select/continue a model. No model weights are uploaded here.
Each of the six source nodes is linked by a fixed verified commit and archived receipt.
